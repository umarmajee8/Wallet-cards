Terms

Written to be understood, not relied on in court. If this is about to go on an app store, have the owner review these words and the repository’s licence first — the site README says so too.

What you are downloading

The APK linked from this site is a debug-signed test build: it exists so reviewers and the owner can put it on a phone. It is not a store release, it carries no promise of continued support, and the signing key is a throwaway one. Do not sell it or hand it out as a release.

No warranty, in plain words

The app is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. It has no server, so there is no service level to promise and nobody who can restore your data for you. Keep a backup before you uninstall, upgrade or change phones.

Your data, your responsibility

  • You are responsible for the .cwbak backup file and its password. Lose the password and the backup is unreadable — that is the design, not a bug we can undo.
  • You are responsible for the security of the phone it runs on. A locked app does not protect a screen recording, a shoulder-surfer, or a rooted device.
  • Hold documents that belong to you, or that you are allowed to hold. A forgotten 4-digit code means Reset app, which erases everything on that device.
  • Card customisation is switched off by default: the design controls appear only while the “Customise cards” switch is on, and that switch turns itself off again when you close Settings.

What the app will never claim

It will not ask for a CVV or a card PIN. It will not collect analytics. It will not sync your cards to a server, because there is no server. If any of that changes, these pages and the app change together, before the release.

Feedback

Bugs, security reports and questions: the issue tracker. A report about a way to read somebody else’s cards is welcome there too.
